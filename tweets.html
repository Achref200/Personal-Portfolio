    Platform · Nesty · Live with partners

    Nesty is live — and it's a lot more than a website.

    Four connected surfaces on one data model: a public marketplace, a mobile app for seekers and hosts, an agency workspace for professional listers managing whole portfolios, and an admin console for moderation, verification, and payouts.

    The hard part wasn't any single screen — it was role-based access across four very different audiences. A renter on a phone, an agency managing sixty units, and an operator moderating the marketplace all need completely different tools without it feeling like three separate products.

    Now onboarding real agencies and partners in Tunisia. Watching people actually list properties on something you designed hits different.

  • Achref Ben Yaagoub

    @achrefby · 2026

    B2B SaaS · Voyagi · Onboarding agencies

    Shipping Voyagi — a multi-tenant SaaS for travel agencies. Every agency gets its own isolated workspace: their catalogue, their branding, their sellers, their commission rules. Clients book under the agency's name, not ours.

    Three surfaces: the agency back-office (catalogue, booking engine, reservations, invoices, revenue dashboards), a mobile seller app where collaborators quote clients on the spot and watch their commission accrue live, and a super-admin console for onboarding, subscriptions, feature flags, and cross-tenant analytics.

    The commission engine is the piece I'm proudest of — configurable rates by product, seller, and tier, reconciled every period. It's what turns a scattered network of freelance sellers into a measurable distribution channel.

    Working directly with agency owners and partners to shape the roadmap. Building B2B software teaches you fast that the prettiest screen loses to the one that closes the books correctly.
